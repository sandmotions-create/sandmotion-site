Core digital twin services

From raw CAD file to field-ready deployment.

Four disciplines, run as one continuous pipeline. Each stage narrows the gap between an engineering drawing and a working digital twin your teams can actually use.

I

CAD & BIM Asset Optimization

Transforming complex engineering designs into lightweight, real-time 3D models: the foundation every downstream twin is built on.

CAD to Real-Time Conversion
Heavy industrial files from SolidWorks, AutoCAD and Revit, converted into clean assets ready for web and engine integration.
BIM Model Preparation
Building information models cleaned up for facility-management twins without losing architectural detail.
PBR Material & Shader Creation
Physically-based rendering textures so machinery and environments hold up under any lighting condition.
II

Real-Time 3D & WebGL Environments

Interactive, browser-based or engine-powered environments that turn a static model into a space people can move through.

Interactive Web-Based 3D
Three.js and WebGL builds that let stakeholders explore a high-detail facility in the browser, with zero installs.
Immersive Game Engine Builds
Photorealistic, fully walkable environments in Unreal Engine and Unity for factory floors, plants and smart buildings.
3D Spatial UI & Dashboards
Control interfaces with live status overlays, hover cards and interactive callouts placed directly in 3D space.
III

Data Visualization & Dynamic Animation

Turning abstract spreadsheet data into visual motion: the layer that makes a twin worth watching, not just looking at.

Dynamic Data Overlays
Heatmaps, airflow vectors, stress-strain indicators and fluid-dynamics animations mapped onto physical asset models.
Exploded & Assembly Views
Interactive or video-based exploded views revealing internal components, wear points and fluid flow.
Status & Alert Animations
Intuitive state indicators: steady green for nominal, pulsing amber for wear, flashing red for critical failure.
IV

AR / VR & Field Operations

Equipping teams on the ground with spatial, interactive overlays, where the twin leaves the screen and meets the machine.

AR Overlay Models
Assets that let technicians overlay a digital model directly onto physical machinery using tablets or smart glasses.
VR Training Simulators
Hands-on, risk-free training scenarios for expensive or hazardous equipment.
Spatial Maintenance Guides
Step-by-step, animated 3D guides walking field workers through complex repair procedures.
