## 2. Why you should be careful

- Several figures are from vendors who sell tours.
- Some are older, from other countries, or measure views, not sales.
- One 2026 industry review states that the popular 36% conversion boost has not been independently verified.

**What it means.** The direction is consistent: buyers like to explore before they visit. The size of the lift is something you should measure on your own project.

## 3. How to measure your own

1. Use a tracked short link (UTM-tagged or shortened) per channel to count opens.
2. Compare site visits per 100 leads before and after.
3. Compare booking rate for tour-viewers vs non-viewers.

## 4. What to report to your management

Report your own funnel numbers, not borrowed ones. Three clean weeks of data beat any industry statistic.
