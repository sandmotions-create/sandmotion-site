ANAROCK also reports about 6.3 lakh unsold homes (+12%). Knight Frank puts time to sell all inventory at 8.0 quarters, up from 7.3.

## 2. Five ways to sell faster

1. **Make the project understandable in 60 seconds.** A link, not a PDF.
2. **Answer in minutes.** Reply to every enquiry on WhatsApp within 10 minutes.
3. **Qualify before the visit.** Send the walkthrough first; invite only the engaged.
4. **Arm channel partners.** Give them one link per unit type.
5. **Show, don't claim.** True-to-scale 3D beats adjectives.

**What it means.** In a crowded market, buyers shortlist the projects they understand. Clarity is a sales advantage, not a design nicety.

## 3. Where to start this week

Pick your slowest-moving unit type. Build a walkable flat for it. Share it with your top ten channel partners and track what happens.
