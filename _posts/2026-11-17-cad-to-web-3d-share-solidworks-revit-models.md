## 1. The problem with sharing CAD

Sending a SolidWorks or Revit file means large downloads, version confusion and viewers who cannot open it.

## 2. What conversion involves

- **Optimise geometry:** reduce detail that nobody sees.
- **Clean BIM data:** keep the information that matters for facilities.
- **Apply materials:** physically based textures that hold up in any light.
- **Publish:** a link that works in a browser.

## 3. Who benefits

| Team | Use |
|---|---|
| Sales | Customer demos of machines and plants |
| Engineering | Design reviews without CAD licences |
| Operations | Layout and flow reviews |
| Training | Safe, repeatable learning |

## 4. What to prepare

Native or exported CAD or BIM, a list of must-keep parts, and the audience for the output.

**What it means.** A model that only engineers can open helps only engineers. A link opens the conversation to everyone else.
