- Many projects need approval from people who are not buyers: lenders, landowners, joint-venture partners and, in redevelopment, society members who vote.

## Who else needs to see your project

| Stakeholder | Their question | Pack |
|---|---|---|
| Redevelopment society members | What will my new flat be like? | Walkable flat per unit type, old-vs-new area comparison, corpus and timeline summary |
| Landowners and JV partners | Will this be well designed and well sold? | Masterplan view, unit mix, sales approach |
| Lenders and banks | Is this credible and on track? | Approvals, progress views, accurate areas |
| Architects and consultants | Will the design hold up? | Shared model with comments |
| Channel partners | Can I sell it? | Link per unit, fact sheet, scripts |

## Redevelopment: why it is different

In redevelopment, the people who approve the project will live in it. They are comparing a new flat with the home they know. Show them:

- The **new layout** next to the old one.
- **Carpet area** clearly stated and consistent with documents.
- **Light, views and amenities** they did not have.
- A **simple timeline** and what happens to them during construction.

## Keep it consistent

1. One accurate model, versioned.
2. The same registration details and disclaimers in every pack.
3. Clear labels where something is indicative.
4. A single owner who approves changes.
