fäm Properties finds studio sales up 26% in January to August while larger units fell. It also reports that only 44 of 717 off-plan projects had cut prices by 5% or more, so developers are protecting price and competing on clarity.

## 2. Why 3D suits compact units

In a 35 to 50 sq m unit, every wall and door matters. Buyers want to know where the sofa, bed and desk fit. A walkable model answers that in seconds.

## 3. Compliance first

Every property advert in Dubai needs a valid Trakheesi permit number. For off-plan, adverts must also show details such as the developer name, escrow account number and expected completion date. Verify permit status in the Dubai REST app and renew before expiry.

**What it means.** Remote investors compare many launches. Give them a link they can open at midnight in Mumbai, London or Lagos, with the permit number visible.

## 4. Checklist for brokers

- Permit number on every image, video, caption and landing page
- One walkthrough link per unit type
- Layout-efficiency notes in the message, not just price
