## 3. Global context

Grand View Research (June 2026) values the global market at $35.8B in 2025, $49.5B in 2026 and projects $328.5B by 2033 (31.1% CAGR). North America had the largest share in 2025.

## 4. What it means for SMEs

- Big forecasts do not mean every plant needs a full IoT twin.
- Start with a visual twin of one line or asset.
- Use the twin for sales demos, training and design reviews first.

**What it means.** Treat market numbers as direction. Your decision should rest on one problem you can fix and measure.
