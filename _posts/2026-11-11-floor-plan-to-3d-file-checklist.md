## 1. Accepted formats

DWG, DXF, PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 25 MB through the form. Other formats can be sent on WhatsApp and we confirm before you upload.

## 2. What makes a plan "usable"

- Clear walls and openings
- Dimensions or a scale
- Room names
- Door and window positions

## 3. Nice to have

- Ceiling height and slab thickness
- Balcony and terrace details
- Orientation (where the sun comes from)
- Finish preferences: flooring, wall colours, kitchen style
- A furniture layout, if you have one

## 4. One-page checklist

| Item | Required? |
|---|---|
| Floor plan file | Yes |
| Dimensions or scale | Yes |
| Ceiling height | Recommended |
| Orientation | Recommended |
| Finishes / furniture | Optional |
| Unit type name | Yes |

**What it means.** The cleaner the plan, the faster the model. If your plan is messy, send it anyway. We will tell you what is missing.

## 5. What happens next

We model to the dimensions in your plan, confirm the layout with you, then deliver a link. Files are used only to build your walkthrough and are not shared with third parties. We can sign an NDA first.
