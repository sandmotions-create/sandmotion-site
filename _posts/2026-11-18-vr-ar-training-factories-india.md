## 1. Good fits

- **Hazardous tasks:** practise without risk.
- **Expensive or busy equipment:** train without stopping production.
- **Complex repairs:** follow step-by-step 3D guides.
- **Remote field teams:** same training, any location.

## 2. Poor fits

- Simple, low-risk tasks
- Small teams with rare training needs
- Where no one will maintain the content

## 3. VR vs AR

| | VR | AR |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Anywhere, simulated | On the real machine |
| Device | Headset | Tablet or smart glasses |
| Best for | Practice and safety | On-the-job guidance |

## 4. How to pilot

1. Choose one task with a clear before/after.
2. Build a short module.
3. Test with one team.
4. Compare time-to-competence and error rates.

**What it means.** Measure training outcomes, not headset hours. If a module does not change performance, change the module.
