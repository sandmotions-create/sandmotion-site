## 1. The problem

Sales teams spend hours on visits that go nowhere. The buyer was curious, not ready.

## 2. The four-step method

1. **Capture:** name, WhatsApp number, budget band.
2. **Send:** the walkthrough link for the matching unit type, in the first reply.
3. **Watch:** opens (use a tracked short link), replies, questions.
4. **Call and book:** only the leads that engaged.

## 3. A simple score

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Opened the link | 2 |
| Replied with a question | 2 |
| Asked about price or payment plan | 3 |
| Asked for a visit | 4 |
| Went silent for 3 days | -2 |

Call anyone at 5 or more first.

**What it means.** You are not guessing intent. You are reading it from what the buyer chose to do.

## 4. Keep it human

- Reply in the buyer's language.
- Ask one question per message.
- Never send three links at once.

## 5. What to review weekly

Visit-to-booking rate, reply time, and the most common buyer question. That question is your next piece of content.
