## 1. The 12 questions

1. **Can I open a live demo on my phone?** If it needs an app or a headset, expect friction for buyers.
2. **Is the model true to scale?** It should follow the dimensions in your plan.
3. **Will you confirm the layout before final delivery?**
4. **How long for a first demo?** Days, not weeks.
5. **Is there a free or low-cost pilot?**
6. **How are changes priced?** Updating a model should be quicker than starting over.
7. **Are furniture and finishes indicative or specified?** Know before you promise buyers.
8. **How will my files be handled?** Ask for an NDA if needed.
9. **Does it work on older phones?** Ask which devices they test on.
10. **Who owns the final model and link?**
11. **Can the same model give me renders and video?**
12. **Can you handle multiple unit types and floors?**

## 2. Red flags

- Only pre-rendered videos shown as "interactive"
- No live link, only screenshots
- Vague timelines
- Pricing that changes after the pilot

**What it means.** A good vendor makes it easy to test. If you cannot try it before you commit, ask why.
