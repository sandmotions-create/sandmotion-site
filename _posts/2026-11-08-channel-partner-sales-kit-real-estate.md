## 1. Why most kits fail

A 40-page PDF does not get forwarded. A broker on a call needs something that opens in one tap and answers "what does it look like inside?"

## 2. The six-part kit

1. **Walkthrough link** for each unit type.
2. **Three-line pitch:** location, size, one differentiator.
3. **Price and payment plan** as a one-page image.
4. **Compliance block:** RERA number, QR code or Trakheesi number as applicable.
5. **Message template** for WhatsApp.
6. **Visit-booking link** or contact number.

## 3. A WhatsApp template that works

> Hi [Name], here is a walkable 3D view of the 2 BHK at [Project]. Open it on your phone and walk through the rooms: [link]. Want me to hold a visit slot this weekend?

Short, one link, one question.

**What it means.** Brokers are your distribution network. Anything that makes their pitch faster makes your sales faster.

## 4. Rules for follow-up

- Reply to broker questions inside 10 minutes.
- Never send more than one link per message.
- Ask for a visit only after the link is opened.
