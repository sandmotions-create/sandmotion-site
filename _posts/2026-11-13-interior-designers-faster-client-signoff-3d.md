## 1. Where time is lost

Moodboards and stills show a style. They do not show how the room works. Clients then ask for changes after furniture is ordered.

## 2. What to show

- **Layout and circulation:** can two people pass here?
- **Material options:** two flooring or colour choices
- **Light:** how the room looks in morning and evening
- **Scale:** how big the sofa or kitchen island really is

## 3. A sign-off checklist

1. Walk the space with the client.
2. Note decisions in writing.
3. Freeze finishes.
4. Order and install.

## 4. Pair with renders

Use renders for the portfolio and social posts. Use the walkthrough for decision meetings.

**What it means.** Sign-off speed is a profit lever. Every revision you avoid is time you can bill elsewhere.
