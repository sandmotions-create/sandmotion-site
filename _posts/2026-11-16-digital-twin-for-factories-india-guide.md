## 1. What is a digital twin?

A digital twin is a virtual copy of a physical asset. It starts as an accurate 3D model and, when connected to data, shows what the real asset is doing: running, worn or faulty.

## 2. The three layers

1. **Model:** accurate 3D from CAD or BIM.
2. **Data:** sensors, PLC or SCADA feeds, maintenance logs.
3. **Experience:** a 3D screen, dashboard or AR/VR view that people actually use.

## 3. Where to start

- One bottleneck line
- One high-failure machine
- One training problem

Pick something measurable. Prove value in weeks, not years.

## 4. Typical outcomes to aim for

- Faster fault location
- Clearer shop-floor reviews
- Safer, quicker training
- Easier communication with leadership and customers

**What it means.** The question is not 'Do we need a digital twin?' It is 'Which problem would we solve first if we could see our plant in 3D?'

## 5. What it needs from you

CAD or layout files, a list of data sources you already have, and a named owner on your side.
