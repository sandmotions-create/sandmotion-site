## 1. Why pre-launch is hard

You have a plan, a brochure and a render. The buyer has a question: "What will it actually feel like?" Without a sample flat, that question goes unanswered and the visit gets postponed.

## 2. The six-step playbook

1. **Pick your top two unit types.** These carry most of your sales. Model them first.
2. **Share the floor plan.** DWG, DXF, PDF, JPG or PNG all work for a first demo.
3. **Get a walkable 3D flat.** One link, opens on any phone, no app.
4. **Put the link everywhere buyers already are.** WhatsApp broadcast, channel partner groups, ads, email signature, QR on hoardings.
5. **Reply fast.** Call anyone who opened the link, within minutes, not days.
6. **Invite only engaged buyers to the site.** Fewer, better visits.

## 3. Keep it compliant

In Maharashtra, project promotions must carry the MaharERA registration number and QR code. Add a short note that furniture and finishes are indicative. Check your own state RERA rules before you publish.

**What it means.** A pre-launch is a confidence problem, not an information problem. A walkable flat answers the doubt that a brochure cannot.

## 4. What to measure

- Link opens per day (use a tracked short link)
- Site visits per 100 link opens
- Bookings per 100 site visits

If visits per link open rise, the walkthrough is doing its job.
