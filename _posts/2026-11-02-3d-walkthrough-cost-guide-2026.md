Everyone asks the same question first: how much? The honest answer is a range, and it depends on what you are buying. These ranges come from published studio and guide pages in 2026. They are marketing figures, not audited prices, but they show where the market sits.


<div class="scenario"><span class="label">Why quotes differ</span>Two quotes for 'a 3D walkthrough': one is ₹30,000, the other ₹3 lakh. They are not comparing the same thing. One is a short fixed-path video of one unit. The other is an interactive experience of several unit types with views, finishes and hosting. Price is only meaningful once scope is clear.</div>

## India (published ranges, 2026)

| Deliverable | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Furnished 3D floor plan | ₹6 to 12 per sq ft (premium); ₹1 to 6 per sq ft for basic and standard |
| 3D interior render | ₹3,500 to ₹8,000 per room (higher for photoreal luxury) |
| 3D exterior render | ₹4,000 to ₹9,000 per view (₹5,000 to 18,000 with landscaping, night or aerial) |
| Walkthrough video | ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000+ (short pieces); some studios quote ₹50,000 to ₹2,40,000+ per minute |
| One Indian studio's list rate | ₹1,250 to 1,450 per second of walkthrough video |

## Dubai (published ranges, 2026)

| Deliverable | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Interior still | AED 500 to 1,500 (basic); AED 2,000 to 5,000 (premium) |
| Exterior still | AED 2,500 to 8,000; luxury villa AED 6,000 to 20,000 |
| Commercial or tower exterior | AED 8,000 to 50,000+ |
| 60-second walkthrough | AED 10,000 to 25,000 |
| Cinematic film | AED 25,000 to 100,000+ |

## Global

International studios commonly quote roughly $600 to $2,500 per high-quality still and, per one studio guide, about $2,200 to $12,000+ per finished minute of walkthrough animation, depending on detail.

## What interactive web walkthroughs add

A video is a fixed path. An interactive web walkthrough lets the buyer choose where to go, can show sun and views, updates faster when plans change, and can carry analytics. It is usually priced per unit type, plus finish options and hosting. Ranges are not yet standardised, which is why scope and a live demo matter more than a rate card.

## Seven things that move the price

1. **Number of unit types** and floors.
2. **Fidelity level** (see our [quality checklist]({% post_url 2026-03-31-good-is-the-enemy-of-great %})).
3. **Interactivity** (video vs walkable).
4. **Views and site data** (drone plates, accurate sun path).
5. **Revisions** and change requests after delivery.
6. **Speed** (rush work often carries a 20 to 50% premium).
7. **Hosting, analytics and updates.**

## Cost per asset used

Divide the price by the number of assets you will actually use: the walkthrough link, hero stills, a reel, social posts, brochure art, QR codes. One model feeding many outputs is far cheaper than ordering each separately.
