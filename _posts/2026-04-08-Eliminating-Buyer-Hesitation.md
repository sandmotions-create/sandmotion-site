<div class="takeaways" markdown="1">
**Key takeaways**

- Hesitation is usually an unanswered question the buyer did not say aloud.
- Twelve questions cover most cases: space, light, view, privacy, safety, price, timing, family, payment, delivery, resale and trust.
- Match each question to a specific asset (walkthrough, fact sheet, comparison, call) and a script.
- Track which questions come up most, and fix the biggest first.
</div>

<div class="scenario"><span class="label">What 'I will think about it' means</span>The buyer says, 'Let me think about it.' What she means: 'I am not sure the second bedroom is big enough for my parents, but I do not want to say that out loud.' Your job is to make that question easy to answer.</div>

## The 12 silent hesitations

| # | The silent question | Answer with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is it big enough? | Walkthrough with true scale and furniture options |
| 2 | Will it feel bright? | Sun slider and time-of-day views |
| 3 | What will I see? | View plates by floor |
| 4 | Is it private? | Surroundings modelled accurately |
| 5 | Is this legal and safe? | Registration, approvals and permits page |
| 6 | Is the price fair? | Price per carpet sq ft comparison |
| 7 | Will it be delivered on time? | Progress updates and track record |
| 8 | Will my family like it? | Share link and a family call |
| 9 | Can I afford the payment plan? | Simple payment-plan calculator |
| 10 | What if the plan changes? | Clear terms and a change policy |
| 11 | Will it hold value? | Honest market context, not promises |
| 12 | Can I trust this developer? | Fast replies, transparent documents, verified details |
