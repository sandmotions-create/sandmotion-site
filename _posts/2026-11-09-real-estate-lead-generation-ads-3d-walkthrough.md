## 1. Why the creative matters

Most property ads look alike: a tower render, a price, a button. A short clip that moves through a living room and ends on "Walk through it yourself" stands out and sets the right expectation.

## 2. The simple funnel

1. **Ad:** 10 to 15 second clip cut from the 3D model.
2. **Click:** to WhatsApp or a two-field form.
3. **Reply:** the walkthrough link, plus one question.
4. **Call:** anyone who opens it or replies.
5. **Visit:** only the engaged.

## 3. Channels

- **Meta (Instagram, Facebook):** click-to-WhatsApp or lead forms. Best for reach and visual creative.
- **Google Search:** people already searching. Target phrases like "2 BHK in [locality]" and "[project name]".
- **YouTube and Reels:** short walkthrough clips, link in description or CTA.
- **Channel partners:** the same link, forwarded.

## 4. Creative that works

- Open on the best room in the first two seconds.
- Show real scale and daylight, not just fly-overs.
- End with one action: "Open the 3D flat."
- Keep the RERA number or permit number on screen.

**What it means.** The cheapest lead is not the best lead. A buyer who walked your flat on their phone is worth several who only liked a render.

## 5. Measure the right things

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Cost per lead | Shows ad efficiency only |
| Cost per site visit | Shows lead quality |
| Cost per booking | Shows real return |
| Reply time | The biggest lever you control |

## 6. Compliance

Add the project's RERA registration number (and QR code in Maharashtra). In Dubai, every advert needs a valid Trakheesi permit number. Label renders as artist's impressions where required.
