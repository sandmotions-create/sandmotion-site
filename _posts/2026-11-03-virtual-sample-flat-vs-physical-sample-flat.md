## 1. What each option really costs

A physical sample flat is a small construction project. You pay for the unit, the fit-out, furniture, lighting, security, housekeeping and a sales executive to stand in it. Those costs run for months, and every design change means rebuilding.

A virtual sample flat is built once from your floor plan and then shared as a link. Changing a finish or a layout means updating the existing model, not starting over.

## 2. Time to launch

A physical flat takes weeks to months. A digital sample flat takes days. Sandmotion's free first-floor demo is delivered in 3 to 5 working days after we receive a usable plan.

This matters most for NRI buyers, out-of-city investors and channel partners who will never visit your site office before a first call.

## 4. When you still need a physical flat

Material feel, ceiling height and finish quality are hard to judge on a screen. Many builders use both: the digital flat to generate and qualify leads, the physical flat to close the final few.

**What it means.** Do not choose one. Put the virtual flat first in your funnel so only serious buyers spend time travelling to the physical one.

## 5. A simple decision rule

- Pre-launch or early launch, no flat built yet: go digital first.
- Many unit types: digital, because each type is a model, not a build.
- Premium finish story: digital now, physical when the show unit is ready.
