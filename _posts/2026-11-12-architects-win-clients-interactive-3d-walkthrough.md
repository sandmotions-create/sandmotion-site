## 1. The gap

You see the building in your head. The client sees lines. The result: "Can we see it differently?" at the last minute.

## 2. Where walkthroughs help

- **Pitch:** a link in your proposal email.
- **Concept review:** let the client stand in the living room.
- **Design freeze:** confirm sizes and sightlines before drawings go out.
- **Approvals:** show boards or authorities the intent clearly.

## 3. Workflow

1. Share the 2D plan or CAD with the studio.
2. Get a model with materials and lighting.
3. Review on a phone with the client.
4. Capture comments, update the existing model.

## 4. Package it as a service

Offer "interactive design review" as a line item. Clients value the clarity, and it differentiates you from firms that only send PDFs.

**What it means.** The architect who lets the client walk the design earns trust earlier, and keeps it through construction.
